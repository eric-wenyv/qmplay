# 素材

本目录保存 qqmui（qq music ui，面向 Linux 的极简 QQ 音乐播放器）的内置素材。

- `coast.jpg`：内置 `image_gen` 工具生成，保存为本地 JPEG，用作尚未加载歌曲或真实封面不可用时的占位封面。
- `icon.svg` / `icon.png`：仓库内原生矢量图标及其 PNG 版本。

封面生成提示词：

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square album artwork for a quiet minimalist desktop music player. Create one square fine art 35mm analog photograph of the coastline at dusk: a calm muted blue green sea, dark seafoam waves, a weathered pale sandstone cliff entering from lower left, distant hazy headland on right, huge soft pale yellow and dusty peach sky, one very small burnt orange sun just above the horizon. A sparse, contemplative, beautifully balanced composition, atmospheric film grain, slight halation, rich but very subdued colors. Editorial photography, serene Japanese ambient record aesthetic, natural light. Full bleed square image. No people, no buildings, no borders, no lettering, no logos, no watermark.
